Aberration vs. shape factor

Model, sign convention & teaching notes

Exact meridional intersections and vector Snell refraction at both spherical surfaces. Radii are recomputed with the thick-lens lensmaker equation to keep the selected paraxial back focal length fixed as shape or index changes. Effective focal length varies with the principal-plane position. BFL is measured from the rear vertex; the focus close-up uses that same origin. Longitudinal SA = paraxial BFL − marginal BFL. The minimum button searches q ∈ [−2, 2] at increments of 0.005 using the current beam radius, index, and selected BFL. It minimizes |longitudinal SA|, not wavefront error or spot size. Flip reverses q and rebuilds the fixed-BFL family.

Rotational symmetry, monochromatic rays, and an on-axis collimated beam are assumed. Diffraction, chromatic aberration, Fresnel losses, and off-axis aberrations are omitted. Diagram axes use different scales to make bending legible; focal-plane ray height is magnified in the close-up. Smaller beam radius reduces geometric aberration, but diffraction is outside this model.
